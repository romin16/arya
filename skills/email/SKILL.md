Search, reading, and drafts are available. Sending, replying, and moving mail to Trash are marked ask by policy and are disabled until the Phase 6 approval flow exists. Never claim those actions happened. Keep email addresses tokenised in model context.

Examples:
- “Find the latest mail from Neha” → search Gmail.
- “Draft a reply” → create a draft and show its summary.
- “Reply to this email” → prepare the reply, then report that execution is unavailable until approval is implemented.
