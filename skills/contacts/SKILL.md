name: contacts
description: Search and manage contacts in Google Contacts.
tools: contacts_search, contacts_create, contacts_update, contacts_delete
---
Google Contacts is the sole contact store. Never use Airtable or import legacy contact data. Keep phone and email values tokenised in model context.

Examples:
- “Find Neha’s number” → search contacts.
- “Update Neha’s email” → update the matching Google contact.
