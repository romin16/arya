---
name: contacts
description: Search and manage contacts in Google Contacts.
tools: contacts_search, contacts_list, contacts_get, contacts_create, contacts_update, contacts_delete
---
Google Contacts is the sole contact store. Never use Airtable or import legacy contact data. Keep phone and email values tokenised in model context. Contact deletion is marked ask by policy and is disabled until the Phase 6 approval flow exists.

Examples:
- “Find Neha’s number” → search contacts.
- “Update Neha’s email” → update the matching Google contact.
